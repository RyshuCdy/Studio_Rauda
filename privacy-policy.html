Privacy Policy

How Studio Rauda handles information submitted through this website.

Last updated: September 14, 2026.

Information we collect

When you subscribe to updates, submit a consultation request or contact Studio Rauda, we may receive information you provide, including your contact details, company information, business objectives and the contents of your message.

How information is used

Information is used to evaluate and respond to enquiries, provide requested updates, manage events and improve our communication. We do not sell personal information.

Service providers

Consultation form responses are processed and stored by Zoho Forms. The website may also use third-party providers to host the website and measure performance. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices.

Your choices

You can request access, correction or deletion of information you have provided, or unsubscribe from marketing communications, by emailing contact@studiorauda.com.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Studio Rauda at contact@studiorauda.com.
